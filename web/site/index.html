Questions worth asking

Let’s talk
about Basis AI.

Good tools should make room
for a healthy amount of skepticism.

What happens when Basis AI gets something wrong?+

Basis AI can produce incorrect or incomplete results. Basis brings source material and suggested results into a review workflow so an engineer can compare, correct, and make the decision. A suggestion still needs judgment.

Does Basis replace the cost segregation engineer?+

No. Basis is being built to assist with organizing information, extracting details, and reviewing property components. Engineering judgment and responsibility remain with the people doing the study.

Why not just use a drive and spreadsheets?+

Folders store files. A study needs the context around them, too. Basis brings source documents, room photographs, component review, and study history together, so your team can return to the work without piecing it back together across applications.

What goes into a study?+

Your appraisal and property photographs. Basis keeps uploaded materials within the study, alongside the room inventory and engineering review, so you can return to the evidence as you work.

Can I review and correct the results?+

Yes. Review extracted property details, inspect room inventories, and check quantities and classifications. Where something needs to change, the workflow supports corrections before the stage moves forward.

How do I access Basis?+

Workspace access is by invitation. If your team already has access, use Sign in with the account connected to your workspace.
