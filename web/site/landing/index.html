Questions you should ask.

Does basis replace me?

No. basis isn’t an automation tool, and it isn’t built to do your job. It takes the busy work: sorting photos, counting components, storing every file and finding it again later. You do the engineering, faster.

Where does basis get its data?

The same places you do. Costs come from RSMeans, adjusted to the property’s year and location. Recovery classes come from the IRS’s own guidance, quoted word for word. The property’s facts come from the appraisal and photos you upload.

Can I change what basis drafts?

Anything. Every count, cost and class is yours to edit, and basis keeps a record of what changed and why. basis was built to never guess: when it isn’t sure, it stops and asks you.

How do I get access?

Access is by invitation for now. Book a demo and we’ll set up a workspace for your firm. If your firm already has one, sign in.
