photo are outdoor_lighting; an outlet is one receptacle plate (switches are not outlets); a
kitchen cabinet unit is one door or one drawer stack; a sink includes its faucet; a garage door
includes its opener. Count windows and doors of neighbouring buildings or vehicles: never.
